# Tren Maya: 12-Day Archaeological Route

A 12-day itinerary through Mexico's Yucatan Peninsula on the Tren Maya railway, hitting the great Maya sites of Palenque, Calakmul, Tulum and Chichen Itza, plus Edzna, Uxmal, Coba, Bacan, Becan, Chicanna and more.

> **Note on "Aztec" sites**: Aztec civilisation was centred in central Mexico (around modern Mexico City). The Tren Maya runs through the Yucatan Peninsula and accesses **Maya** sites exclusively. The closest Aztec-era sites (Templo Mayor in Mexico City, Teotihuacan) are reachable only via a separate domestic flight to Mexico City. This itinerary is entirely Maya.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 - Arrive Cancun

Land, clear immigration, settle in. Light afternoon: El Rey archaeological zone (in the hotel zone, small but uncrowded), Mayan Museum of Cancun. Evening beach, dinner.

- **Train**: none today
- **Sleep**: Cancun
- **Sites**: El Rey, San Miguelito (optional)

### Day 2 - Tulum

Morning train Cancun -> Tulum (~2h). Tulum ruins - arrive at opening (8am) to beat the crowds and midday heat. Clifftop Caribbean views, Temple of the Frescoes. Afternoon: Gran Cenote or Cenote Calavera swim.

- **Train**: Cancun -> Tulum
- **Sleep**: Tulum pueblo (better value than beach zone)
- **Sites**: Tulum, Muyil (optional)

### Day 3 - Coba and Bacalar

Early morning Coba ruins (short taxi/tour from Tulum, ~45 min). Climb Nohoch Mul - one of the last climbable Maya pyramids in the region, 42m tall with jungle canopy views. Afternoon train Tulum -> Bacalar (~3h). Late afternoon swim in the Lagoon of Seven Colours.

- **Train**: Tulum -> Bacalar
- **Sleep**: Bacalar
- **Sites**: Coba

### Day 4 - Bacalar to Xpujil

Morning kayak or boat trip on the lagoon (Canal de los Piratas, Cenote Negro). Afternoon train Bacalar -> Xpujil (~4h). On arrival: book tomorrow's Calakmul tour through your hotel or a local agency (~1,600-1,700 MXN per person). Withdraw cash - no ATMs in the biosphere reserve.

- **Train**: Bacalar -> Xpujil (via Chetumal)
- **Sleep**: Xpujil
- **Sites**: none (travel day)

### Day 5 - Calakmul (UNESCO)

Depart 6-7am. 2-hour drive through the biosphere reserve (watch for spider monkeys, toucans, coatis crossing the road). On site: Structure II is one of the largest Maya pyramids ever built (55m), flanked by two tombs. Climb it for an unbroken jungle horizon in every direction. 3-4 hours exploring. Return to Xpujil by late afternoon. This is the day you came for.

- **Train**: none
- **Sleep**: Xpujil
- **Sites**: **Calakmul** (~110km from Xpujil, private taxi or guided tour required)
- **Entry fees**: ~400 MXN total for foreigners (Conhuas community + biosphere reserve + archaeological site). Cash only.

### Day 6 - Becan, Chicanna, onwards to Palenque

Morning: Becan (2,000-year-old moated city, panoramic views from Structure IX) and Chicanna (famous monster-mouth doorway, Structure II). Short taxi hops from Xpujil. Afternoon train Xpujil -> Palenque (~5-6h - longest leg; bring snacks, water, a book).

- **Train**: Xpujil -> Palenque
- **Sleep**: Palenque town
- **Sites**: Becan, Chicanna (and Xpujil Ruins, Balamku if time)

### Day 7 - Palenque

Full day at Palenque ruins. Temple of the Inscriptions (Pakal's tomb), the Palace (four-storey tower, unique in Maya architecture), Temple of the Cross complex. The site is embedded in jungle - howler monkeys roaring overhead, mist in the trees. Arrive early before the heat and day-trip crowds.

- **Train**: none
- **Sleep**: Palenque
- **Sites**: **Palenque** (all day)

### Day 8 - Waterfalls and Campeche

Morning: Agua Azul (cascading turquoise pools, 1.5h from Palenque) or Misol-Ha (35m single-drop waterfall you can walk behind, 45 min away). Afternoon train Palenque -> Campeche (~5h).

- **Train**: Palenque -> Campeche
- **Sleep**: Campeche
- **Sites**: Agua Azul or Misol-Ha waterfalls

### Day 9 - Edzna and Campeche

Morning: Edzna ruins (short taxi from Campeche). The Pyramid of the Five Storeys, a massive acropolis, and an advanced canal system - nearly empty compared to Chichen Itza. Afternoon: Campeche historic centre. Pastel-coloured colonial streets, intact city walls (UNESCO), malecon walk at sunset. Excellent seafood for dinner.

- **Train**: none
- **Sleep**: Campeche
- **Sites**: **Edzna**

### Day 10 - Merida

Morning train Campeche -> Merida (~2.5h). Afternoon: Gran Museo del Mundo Maya (world-class), Paseo de Montejo, Plaza Grande, cathedral. Merida is safe, walkable, and the cultural heart of the Yucatan.

- **Train**: Campeche -> Merida
- **Sleep**: Merida
- **Sites**: none (city day; optionally Dzibilchaltun nearby)

### Day 11 - Chichen Itza

Day trip to Chichen Itza (~1.5h by road from Merida, or use the Tren Maya station). Arrive at 8am opening - by 10am the tour buses from Cancun arrive en masse. El Castillo, the Great Ball Court (largest in Mesoamerica), Temple of the Warriors, the Sacred Cenote. Afternoon: cool off at Cenote Ik Kil (5 min away) or continue to Ek Balam (45 min north - extraordinary stucco facade, far fewer visitors).

- **Train**: Merida -> Chichen Itza (or road day trip)
- **Sleep**: Merida
- **Sites**: **Chichen Itza**, optionally Ek Balam, Cenote Ik Kil

### Day 12 - Fly Home

Morning Merida: last-minute mercado shopping, cochinita pibil breakfast. Afternoon: Merida airport (MID) -> Mexico City (MEX), ~2h. Evening: MEX -> LHR direct, ~10h 45m overnight.

## Practical Tips

- **Cash**: Many archaeological sites are cash-only (Mexican pesos). ATMs exist in Xpujil and Campeche but not inside reserves. Bring enough cash in Xpujil before heading to Calakmul.
- **Train bookings**: Schedules change. Check trenmaya.gob.mx or buy at station counters 1-2 days ahead. Trains are generally reliable but departures can be limited (1-3 per day on some sections).
- **Calakmul station trap**: Never disembark at the "Calakmul" station - it is isolated with no services or taxis. Use **Xpujil station only**.
- **Calakmul logistics**: The final 110km from Xpujil to the ruins is through jungle - part of the experience. No public transport covers it. Hire a guided tour (~1,600 MXN pp, min 2) or a private taxi. Bring water, snacks, sunscreen, bug spray. 3-4 hours on site.
- **Heat and sun**: Palenque and Calakmul are deep jungle - humid and hot. Bring bug spray, sunscreen, a wide-brimmed hat, and drink plenty of water. Start early (ruins open at 8am).
- **Spanish**: Very little English spoken outside Cancun/Tulum. Useful phrases:
  - "Un boleto para..." = one ticket to...
  - "A que hora sale?" = what time does it leave?
  - "Cuanto cuesta?" = how much does it cost?
  - "Donde esta el cajero automatico?" = where is the ATM?
- **Bug spray and sunscreen**: Palenque and Calakmul are deep jungle. Bring both.
- **Clothing**: Lightweight, breathable, long sleeves/trousers for jungle sites to reduce bug bites. Comfortable walking shoes with grip (uneven stone steps).

## Tren Maya Route Reference

The Tren Maya is ~1,554 km of railway forming a loop through five states (Quintana Roo, Yucatan, Campeche, Tabasco, Chiapas).

| Section | Route | Distance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Palenque - Escarcega | 227 km |
| 2 | Escarcega - Calkini | 219 km |
| 3 | Calkini - Izamal | 157 km |
| 4 | Izamal - Cancun | 239 km |
| 5 | Cancun - Tulum | 111 km |
| 6 | Tulum - Chetumal | 256 km |
| 7 | Chetumal - Escarcega | 256 km |

Our itinerary follows this loop counter-clockwise: Cancun -> Tulum -> Bacalar -> Xpujil -> Palenque -> Campeche -> Merida -> Chichen Itza, exiting at Merida for the flight to Mexico City.
